Before you choose

A few useful
answers.

Can you help me check compatibility?+

Yes. The intended shop experience lets you send your basket with details of your boat and existing equipment. The team can then review the proposed setup, including power requirements, available space and integration.

Can I buy a bundle and have it fitted?+

The bundles are a starting point for a conversation. Installation would be quoted separately after the team has reviewed your boat, existing equipment and the scope of work.

What happens if I only need one part?+

You can add individual products to your basket. You can also ask for advice before committing to a wider system.

Can I place a real order in this preview?+

This is a local design mockup. The catalogue, prices and recommendations are illustrative. You can try the basket and save an enquiry preview, but no order, payment or message will be sent.
