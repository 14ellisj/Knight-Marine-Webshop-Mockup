KNIGHT MARINE — WEBSHOP MOCKUP
================================

OPEN THE MOCKUP
1. Unzip Knight-Marine-Webshop-Mockup.zip.
2. Open the extracted Knight-Marine-Webshop-Mockup folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in a modern browser.

Keep index.html, styles.css, shop.js and the assets folder together.
No server, installation, internet connection or account is needed.
For a phone-sized preview, narrow your desktop browser window.

WHAT TO TRY
- Browse eight illustrative products across five equipment categories.
- Search by product or brand; filter categories and sort by price or name.
- Open product details and add equipment to the basket.
- Follow the three-step Find your setup guide.
- Explore three equipment bundles, see their contents and add them to the basket.
- Change basket quantities or remove products.
- Try demo checkout. Use sample details, select collection or example delivery,
  review the total and complete a demo order.
- Ask the team to review the basket, or discuss an advisor recommendation.
- Create an enquiry preview and save a text copy to your computer.
- Open the FAQs and try the mobile navigation at a narrow screen width.

DESIGN DIRECTION
The concept carries forward Knight Marine's deep navy, bright blue and muted
gold palette, large closely spaced headings, rounded cards, pill buttons,
marina photography and personal, practical tone. It combines equipment sales
with advice on bundles, compatibility and professional fitting.

IMPORTANT CONCEPT DETAILS
This is a design mockup, not a live shop. Products, descriptions, prices,
delivery costs and bundle recommendations are illustrative. Manufacturer
names are examples; exact models, specifications, stock, compatibility,
tax treatment and commercial terms have not been verified.
Product illustrations are original SVG concept drawings, not manufacturer
product photography. Boat and installation images come from the existing
Knight Marine project.

Bundles are examples of a conversation starter, not complete installation
specifications. Supporting components and fitting are excluded. Bundle prices
are the sum of the shown items; no discount is assumed.

No payments, emails, orders, uploads or network requests are made.
Checkout uses a demo payment step and never asks for card information.
Completing the demo checkout clears the basket.

The basket is stored in this browser where local-file storage is supported.
If storage is unavailable, it still works for the current page session.
Contact and checkout details are not stored by the mockup; closing their
preview clears those fields. A text enquiry is saved only if you choose the
Save enquiry button. Browser autofill behavior depends on browser settings.
